# Delivery-risk response

Investigate an open order when the saved delivery-risk model exceeds its validation-selected alert threshold. Review warehouse workload, shipping distance, supplier lead time, and the service promise. A score is an estimated probability under synthetic training conditions, not proof that an order will fail.

For warehouse congestion, inspect staffing and dispatch queues before promising faster service. For long-distance orders, verify carrier pickup and realistic service windows. Escalate supplier issues to procurement after checking open purchase orders. The platform cannot change shipments or contact customers automatically.

Scenario results show how the fitted model changes when selected inputs change. They do not estimate a causal intervention. Correlated inputs, unmeasured staffing, and stock availability can invalidate a hypothetical scenario. Use the simulator to prioritize investigations, not to claim guaranteed savings.
