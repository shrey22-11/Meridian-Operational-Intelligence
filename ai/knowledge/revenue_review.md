# Revenue review and management cadence

Compare complete calendar months. Separate region-level revenue movements into order-volume effects and average-basket effects. The exact bridge uses change in order count times previous average order value plus current order count times change in average order value. This explains the arithmetic, not the business cause.

When revenue falls, review acquisition, cancellations, category mix, pricing, discounts, and fulfillment constraints. The dataset has no advertising-spend, competitor-price, or customer-satisfaction measurements, so marketing effectiveness and competitor effects cannot be established. Mark these as hypotheses requiring additional data.

Demand forecasts predict net booked units across the portfolio. The model is selected against a weekly seasonal baseline on chronological validation data. Forecast bands use historical validation errors and widen heuristically over the recursive horizon. They are planning ranges with unverified multi-day coverage.
