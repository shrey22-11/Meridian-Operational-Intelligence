# Business metrics and time conventions

Booked revenue is quantity multiplied by selling unit price after discount. Cancelled orders are excluded. Open orders are included, so booked revenue is neither recognized accounting revenue nor cash receipts. Currency is Indian rupees (INR). Gross margin subtracts product acquisition cost only; it excludes shipping, payroll, refunds, and taxes.

Delivery delay means actual delivery days exceed the promised delivery days. The delay-rate denominator includes delivered orders only. Open orders have no observed delivery outcome. Recent cohorts may show optimistic rates because slower orders remain open; use mature cohorts for comparisons.

The demonstration dataset is synthetic and ends on 2025-12-31. Relative periods refer to the dataset snapshot, not the computer's current date. Last month in the monthly comparison means the last fully observed calendar month, December 2025. Default dashboard metrics cover the last 30 observed days. These are different period definitions.
